## 1. Decorative box-drawing separators (`═══`)

**Scale:** 266 occurrences across 25 files in `public/js`/`lib`/`server.js`/
`test_suite.js`, plus 50 in `public/css/main.css` and 10 in `public/index.html` — 326
total. Top offenders: `public/js/38-ai-autonomous-trading.js` (50 lines / 25 banner
pairs), `public/js/20-wealth.js` (18), `public/js/13-realdata.js` (18),
`public/js/34-ksei-shareholders.js` (17), `public/js/00-config.js` (16),
`lib/idx-data-engine.js` (16).

**Pattern** (e.g. `public/js/38-ai-autonomous-trading.js:23-25`):

```js
// ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
// 1. STATE & ISOLATED PAPER TRADING UNIVERSE
// ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

A three-line box (top rule, ALL-CAPS label, bottom rule) around a section name. This is
the "Decorative Separators" pattern verbatim (antislop-code: "the decoration is the
message... signals 'AI made this' without adding information"). It is mechanical and
consistent enough to be a safe, low-risk fix: strip the two `═══` rule lines, keep the
label as one plain comment line, sentence case optional (the label itself is usually
informative — e.g. "State & isolated paper trading universe" — only the box and the
shouting case are the problem).

**Fix direction:** for each occurrence, delete the two banner lines and keep the label
line as a single `// ...` comment. Pure comment-text edit — no code, identifiers, or
logic touched, per this skill's scope guardrail. Given the volume (326 across ~27
files), I'd do this as one mechanical pass per file rather than one-by-one review.

## 2. Long "FIX (date, user-reported: ...)" comments — flagged, not recommended, conflicts with this repo's own CLAUDE.md

**Scale:** widespread — dozens of these across `public/js/*.js`, typically 5-20 lines
each, documenting a bug, its root cause, what was verified, and what changed.

**Why this skill would normally flag it:** antislop-code's "Over-Explained Comment"
pattern says to cut a comment to the constraint alone, one line, two at most, and calls
out exactly this shape — "citing the issue number and the version that fixed it" — as
the kind of flourish to drop.

**Why I am not recommending it:** this repo's own `CLAUDE.md` (rule #4) explicitly
addresses these same comments and says the opposite:

> `INCIDENT_LOG.md` SUDAH DIHAPUS ... Referensi `INCIDENT_LOG.md` yang masih tersisa di
> komentar kode lama (banyak file `public/js/*.js`) adalah jejak historis, bukan
> instruksi aktif — **tidak perlu diburu dan dihapus satu-satu**.

The project owner already made this exact call: these comments are intentional
historical record (root-cause traces for bugs that were fixed, several of them for
fabricated-data incidents this project cares about a lot), and explicitly said not to
hunt them down and trim them one by one. Trimming them to satisfy this skill's default
would directly reverse that stated decision.

Per the antislop core's own instruction for exactly this situation ("if direction itself
asks for a named pattern this filter would flag, do not silently follow and do not
silently override — name the element, name the rule, ask the owner"): naming it here.
**My recommendation is to leave these alone.** If you want them trimmed anyway, say so
explicitly and I will scope that separately — it is a much larger, higher-judgment edit
than finding #1 (326 mechanical deletions vs. rewriting dozens of comments' content,
which risks losing the history CLAUDE.md asked to keep).
